# NON-NEGOTIABLE DEVELOPMENT RULES
# CAR'S ENERGY CO. WEBSITE

These requirements override any default AI design or development preferences.

Act as an experienced professional software development team. Build a genuine, polished, reliable corporate website. Do not make it look like an automatically generated AI template.

IMPORTANT: Quality, accuracy, usability and authenticity are more important than development speed.

==================================================
1. STRICTLY PROHIBITED DESIGN ELEMENTS
==================================================

NEVER use:

- Purple gradients.
- Purple-blue gradient backgrounds.
- Unnecessary multicolour gradients.
- Pill-shaped buttons.
- Excessively rounded buttons.
- Fake customer reviews.
- Fake testimonials.
- Fake customer counters.
- Fake statistics or business metrics.
- Unverified company achievements.
- Vague hero text.
- Generic marketing slogans.
- Emoji icons.
- AI-generated-looking decorative illustrations.
- AI-slop stock photography.
- AI-slop website copy.
- Fake workshop photographs.
- Fake company logos.
- Fake certifications.
- Fake customer photographs.
- Fake partner brands.
- Artificial cursor animations.
- Custom animated mouse pointers.
- Crazy scroll animations.
- Excessive parallax effects.
- Unnecessary 3D effects.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
- Unnecessary loading animations.
- Fake progress indicators.
- Fake notifications.
- Fake appointment confirmations.
- Placeholder buttons presented as functional.

Do not introduce these elements anywhere in the website.

==================================================
2. BUTTON AND COMPONENT DESIGN
==================================================

Use professional rectangular or subtly rounded buttons.

Button design:
- Consistent height.
- Clear typography.
- Appropriate spacing.
- Subtle corner radius.
- Clear hover state.
- Accessible contrast.
- Visible keyboard focus.

Avoid pill-shaped buttons.

Use consistent spacing, alignment, typography and component sizing.

Use professional SVG icons or a consistent icon library.

Never use emojis as interface icons.

==================================================
3. AUTHENTIC PHOTOGRAPHY AND CONTENT
==================================================

Never use AI-slop photos or AI-generated-looking automotive photographs.

Never fabricate:
- Workshop interiors.
- Company facilities.
- Employees.
- Customers.
- Vehicle installations.
- Product photographs.
- Company events.

Priority for images:

1. Genuine company photographs.
2. Genuine workshop photographs.
3. Authentic manufacturer product images with permission.
4. Properly licensed professional stock photographs.

If genuine photographs are unavailable:
- Use neutral, professional visual placeholders.
- Clearly identify images requiring replacement.
- Never pretend that placeholder images are real company photographs.

COPYWRITING RULES:

Write like a knowledgeable human business writer.

Avoid:
- Generic AI marketing language.
- Repeated promotional phrases.
- Empty claims.
- Unnecessary adjectives.
- Keyword stuffing.
- Robotic descriptions.
- Repetitive content.

Every page must provide useful information to actual customers.

Use clear, natural English and Assamese.

Do not use em dashes ( - ) anywhere in website copy, documentation or interface text. Use commas, full stops or ordinary hyphens instead.

==================================================
4. NO FAKE REVIEWS OR METRICS
==================================================

This is a strict rule.

Never invent:
- Customer review counts.
- Star ratings.
- Number of installations.
- Number of satisfied customers.
- Years of experience.
- Success percentages.
- Service statistics.
- Awards.
- Certifications.
- Customer testimonials.

Only publish verified information supplied by the company or supported by reliable evidence.

If information cannot be verified:
- Leave it out.
- Do not estimate it.
- Do not generate replacement figures.

Do not create animated counters using invented numbers.

If no verified testimonials are available, remove the testimonials section or clearly explain how customers can submit genuine feedback.

==================================================
5. HERO SECTION RULES
==================================================

Do not use vague promotional hero text such as:
- Driving the Future.
- Redefining Excellence.
- Powering Tomorrow.
- Your Journey Starts Here.
- Innovation Meets Performance.

The first screen must immediately explain:

- What Car's Energy Co. does.
- What services are available.
- How customers can contact the company.

Use a compact, informative introduction.

Prioritise business clarity over promotional decoration.

Do not force users to scroll through a large hero section to find useful information.

==================================================
6. ANIMATION RULES
==================================================

Use balanced, professional animations only.

Allowed:
- Subtle scroll reveals.
- Simple hover transitions.
- Smooth navigation dropdowns.
- Lightweight gallery transitions.
- Natural accordion animations.
- Small button interactions.
- Subtle page transitions.

Not allowed:
- Crazy scrolling effects.
- Scroll hijacking.
- Forced horizontal scrolling.
- Excessive parallax.
- Animated cursors.
- Flashing elements.
- Continuous distracting motion.
- Large unnecessary animation libraries.
- Animations that delay access to content.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

The website must remain fully usable when animations are disabled.

==================================================
7. REMOVE ALL AI BRANDING
==================================================

Remove any unwanted AI-generation branding from the website.

Specifically inspect:
- Made with AI.
- Built with AI.
- AI-generated website labels.
- AI template badges.
- Unwanted generator watermarks.
- Default development branding.
- Unwanted template credits.

Remove such labels when they are part of the website's own source code or design.

Do not remove legitimate legal notices, software licences, third-party attribution or hosting-platform notices that must remain.

The finished public website must present Car's Energy Co. as its own professional business website.

==================================================

Create complete and accessible legal pages.

Include:

- Purpose of collecting information.
- How personal information is used.
- How information is stored.
- Data protection measures.
- Data retention.
- Third-party service providers.
- Contact information for privacy questions.
- User rights and applicable legal requirements.

Do not claim compliance or certifications without evidence.

B. TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Include:
- Website usage.
- Accuracy of information.
- Service descriptions.
- Appointment enquiries.
- Appointment confirmation.
- Pricing and quotations.
- Service limitations.
- Intellectual property.
- Third-party links.
- Liability and disclaimers.
- Changes to terms.
- Contact information.

Legal content must be written in clear language and reviewed for applicable Indian legal requirements before publication.

Do not invent a legal entity name, registration number or legal address.

Include the last-updated date.

Add links to both pages in the website footer.

Provide English and Assamese versions.
